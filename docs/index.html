      How the agents talk

      The real pipeline, end to end. Agents communicate only through the shared BAND room.

      Stage 1
      Market Analyst
      Computes SMA/RSI context from delayed daily bars; posts only state-changing signals as room mentions.
      Stage 2
      BAND room
      Shared agent-to-agent channel. Every signal, status and fill is an auditable room message.
      Stage 3
      Trade Executor
      Risk-checks each signal: no shorting, position and cash caps. Rejects or books.
      Stage 4
      Paper portfolio
      Local simulated book. Fills are paper only - nothing touches a brokerage.
