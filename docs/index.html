SignalFloor

An agent-to-agent paper trading desk for NSE stocks. Three moves:

1
Scan the watchlist

An analyst rule set (RSI zones + SMA trend) reads real delayed daily bars and calls BUY, SELL or HOLD - with the reason on the card.

2
Replay before you trust

Run your exact rules over real history bar by bar and watch where they would have bought and sold. Tune the rules in the lab and the chart redraws from the actual computation.

3
Paper trade the plan

Queue an order and the executor books it at a future session's open once that bar arrives. Track cash, holdings and P&L in the paper book.

Simulation only. Delayed end-of-day data, no brokerage, no real orders, no real money. Simulated results are not evidence of real-world performance.
