How it works

A Market Analyst agent computes SMA/RSI signals from delayed daily bars and posts them to a BAND room. A Trade Executor agent listening in the same room risk-checks each signal (no shorting, position and cash caps) and books simulated fills on this paper portfolio. Agents communicate only through the BAND room. Source: github.com/MrNagabhushana-0-dev/signalfloor
